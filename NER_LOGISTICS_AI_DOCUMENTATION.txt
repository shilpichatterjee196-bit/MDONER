1. PROJECT OVERVIEW
================================================================================
Project Name:
NER Logistics AI (SIH26002) - Smart Multi-Modal Logistics & Remote District
Accessibility Intelligence Platform.

Project Purpose:
To provide an integrated, real-time spatial intelligence and operations command
dashboard designed specifically for the unique geographical, topological, and
climatic challenges of India's North Eastern Region (NER). It enables civil
administrators, disaster management teams, logistics coordinators, and fleet
operators to track freight convoys, monitor active landslides and floods, calculate
multi-modal routes (road, Brahmaputra NW-2 riverway, drone air corridors), and
evaluate remote district supply-chain accessibility.

Problem Being Solved:
The North Eastern Region of India faces chronic logistical bottlenecks caused by
harsh Himalayan terrain, fragile geology prone to sudden landslides and rockfalls,
monsoon river flooding and sandbank siltation along the Brahmaputra, sub-zero
black ice on high-altitude passes, and isolated single-lane road lifelines with zero
redundancy. Conventional logistics platforms assume predictable highway networks,
fail to integrate multi-modal alternatives, and do not model terrain accessibility
friction for critical supplies (vaccines, food rations, heating fuel).

Target Users:
1. Central & State Logistics Dispatchers (Food Corporation of India, State Civil Supplies).
2. Emergency & Disaster Response Agencies (SDRF, NDRF, Border Roads Organisation - BRO).
3. North Eastern Council (NEC) & Ministry of Development of North Eastern Region (MoDONER).
4. Fleet Managers & Convoy Commanders (All-terrain 6x6/4x4 trucks, Ro-Pax river barges).
5. Ground Personnel & Commercial Drivers (Crowdsourced hazard reporters, river pilots).

Real-World Use Case:
During monsoon season, a critical mudslide closes NH-29 between Dimapur and Kohima,
stranding essential food trucks. A conventional system only detects a traffic jam.
NER Logistics AI immediately flags the active hazard, evaluates the Remote District
Accessibility Index (RDAI) impact on Kohima, calculates a multi-modal detour via
rural bypasses or river barge transshipment, alerts the convoy commander via SatCom,
and generates an operational dispatch order in under two minutes.

Main Objectives:
- Provide real-time GIS situational awareness across all 8 NER states.
- Enable intelligent multi-modal route planning integrating highway, river, and drone.
- Dynamically assess remote district vulnerability through a composite accessibility score.
- Expedite disaster triage and machinery deployment for road clearance.
- Empower ground drivers and local volunteers to report live hazards with community verification.
- Provide executive SLA analytics on freight modal shift, carbon offsets, and transit time.

Why the Platform is Useful for the North Eastern Region:
NER's topography cannot be solved by standard road logistics alone. By treating the
Brahmaputra National Waterway-2 (NW-2) and VTOL cargo drones as first-class transit
modalities alongside all-terrain heavy vehicles, the platform turns geographical
obstacles into strategic supply corridors.

Major Capabilities of the Platform:
1. Executive Command Center: Live KPI stat counters, active convoy telemetry, terrain hazards feed.
2. Interactive Leaflet GIS Map: Real-time GPS markers, route polylines, elevation markers, hazard radii.
3. Multi-Modal Route Solver: Heuristic engine optimizing for speed, safety, or eco-river transit.
4. Fleet Telematics Registry: Speed, battery/fuel, engine temp, altitude, driver comms, cargo cold-chain.
5. Remote District Accessibility Index (RDAI): Multi-factor scoring with monsoon flood stress simulation.
6. Incident Triage & Emergency SOS: BRO heavy machinery dispatch, clearance progress tracking, broadcast.
7. Crowdsourced Field Hazard Reporting: Driver hazard submissions, photo verification, upvoting.
8. Executive SLA Analytics: Recharts area, bar, and donut charts demonstrating river modal shift.
9. System Configuration: Telemetry polling, NavIC/GSAT-7 satellite selection, offline cache controls.

2. PROBLEM STATEMENT
================================================================================
The Original Logistics / Accessibility Problem:
The North Eastern Region comprises eight states connected to the rest of India
primarily via the narrow 22-kilometer Siliguri Corridor ("Chicken's Neck").
Within the region, physical transportation is severely constrained by rugged
mountain ranges, deep river valleys, seismic instability, and extreme monsoon weather.

Key Geographical & Operational Challenges:
1. Terrain Complexity: Extreme elevation changes (from 50m in the Brahmaputra valley
   to over 4,170m at Sela Pass) place extreme mechanical strain on heavy cargo vehicles.
2. Weather-Related Disruptions: The NER receives among the highest rainfall in the world,
   triggering hundreds of annual landslides, mudslides, and flash floods between May and September.
3. High-Altitude Hazards: High Himalayan passes (Sela Pass, Nathu La) experience sudden
   sub-zero blizzards, freezing fog, and invisible black ice requiring tire chains and 4x4 traction.
4. Single-Lifeline Vulnerability: Multiple state capitals and border districts depend on
   a single highway artery (e.g., NH-29 for Nagaland/Manipur, NH-06 for Barak Valley/Mizoram).
   A single rockfall cuts off all medicine, fuel, and food supplies for weeks.
5. Riverway Neglect: National Waterway-2 (the Brahmaputra River) spans 891 km from Dhubri
   to Sadiya but suffers from shifting sand siltation, lack of night navigation, and poor
   integration with road fleets.
6. Delays in Essential Goods Delivery: Lifesaving cold-chain medicines, antivenom, and
   pediatric vaccines spoil when refrigeration trucks are stalled in mountain bottlenecks.
7. Limitations of Conventional Logistics Systems:
   - Google Maps / Commercial GPS assume standard passenger cars on paved roads.
   - They lack awareness of high-altitude pass closures, Bailey bridge tonnage limits,
     and BRO snow-blower schedules.
   - They do not support multi-modal river barge transfers or cargo drone corridors.
   - They offer zero district-level supply vulnerability modeling.

How NER Logistics AI Addresses This Problem:
The software provides unified visibility into terrain friction, hazard locations,
multi-modal transit options, and district vulnerability. By monitoring road, river,
and skyway simultaneously, it reroutes critical cargo before vehicles enter blocked
corridors, deploys BRO emergency clearance teams faster, and prioritizes life-saving
goods through weather-resilient modalities.

3. SOLUTION OVERVIEW
================================================================================
The platform integrates nine operational modules into a unified web application:

1. Central Operations Dashboard (`/`):
   Real-time overview of regional logistics health, displaying active vehicle
   telemetry, live hazard alerts, key performance indicators (KPIs), high-priority
   corridor status, and quick-dispatch triggers.

2. GIS Spatial Intelligence Map (`/live-map`):
   Interactive Leaflet GIS map with customized markers for all-terrain trucks,
   river barges, cargo drones, and medical rovers; hazard hazard-zones; strategic
   ports, hubs, and high-altitude mountain passes; and animated route polylines.

3. AI Multi-Modal Route Optimization (`/route-optimization`):
   A heuristic decision-support engine that takes origin, destination, cargo type,
   and user constraints (e.g., avoid landslides, allow river barge, permit drone)
   to compute the optimal multi-modal path, showing step-by-step waypoints, risk
   scores, fuel savings, and an alternative direct highway comparison.

4. Vehicle & Fleet Management (`/vehicles`):
   Comprehensive telematics registry tracking GPS location, speed, fuel/battery
   levels, engine temperature, altitude, driver information, direct radio contacts,
   and cargo consignment specifications.

5. Accessibility Intelligence (`/accessibility`):
   The Remote District Accessibility Index (RDAI) dashboard, scoring border districts
   on a 0-100 scale based on terrain complexity, weather vulnerability, road paving
   ratios, bridge redundancy, and lifeline buffer stocks. Includes an interactive
   Monsoon Flood Stress-Test simulator.

6. Alerts & Incident Triage (`/alerts`):
   Emergency incident command feed categorizing natural hazards by severity (Critical,
   Warning, Info) and category (Landslide, Flood, Weather, Infrastructure). Allows
   operators to advance BRO heavy machinery clearance progress and broadcast
   high-priority Emergency SOS alerts.

7. Crowdsourced Field Reporting (`/field-reports`):
   A ground-up intelligence module where convoy drivers, local volunteers, and
   river pilots report road debris, submerged sandbars, or active fissures. Includes
   community upvoting and automatic verification when reports reach 10 votes.

8. SLA Performance & Executive Analytics (`/analytics`):
   Government executive dossier with Recharts visualizations tracking multi-modal
   freight absorption, modal share distribution, hazard delays by type, and state
   accessibility rankings.

9. System Configuration & GIS Settings (`/settings`):
   Customization portal for telemetry ping frequencies, weather radar intervals,
   NavIC/GSAT-7 satellite providers, sound alerts, and offline cache synchronization.

10. Offline & Sync Resilience (`OfflineIndicator`):
    Monitors browser `navigator.onLine` events. When network connectivity drops,
    it informs the operator that submissions and cached routes are stored in
    browser storage and will auto-synchronize upon reconnect.

10. AI ROUTE OPTIMIZATION
================================================================================
1. Nature of the AI System:
   - The route optimization engine is a RULE-BASED HEURISTIC SOLVER simulating an
     intelligent AI multi-modal decision support system.
   - It does NOT make calls to external third-party closed APIs (like OpenAI or Google Cloud AI).
   - It runs deterministic, domain-specific optimization algorithms directly within
     the client application using topological and constraint-based heuristics.

2. User Inputs:
   - Origin Hub (e.g., Guwahati Logistic Hub, Pandu River Port, Silchar Logistics Park).
   - Destination Hub (e.g., Tawang Civil Supply Depot, Imphal Capital Center, Kohima Depot).
   - Cargo Type (Cold-chain vaccines, Disaster food rations, Heating fuel, Agro-fertilizers).
   - Priority Mode:
     - Fastest: Prefers uninterrupted highways or drone skyways.
     - Safest: Avoids sectors with active landslide alerts and steep elevation friction.
     - Eco / River: Maximizes Brahmaputra NW-2 barge transport to reduce fuel consumption.
   - Solver Constraints (Checkboxes):
     - Avoid sectors with >50% landslide risk.
     - Enable Brahmaputra NW-2 barge transit.
     - Permit VTOL drone corridor for cold-chain medicine.

3. Four-Step AI Processing Sequence:
   When the user clicks "Recalculate Corridor", the UI displays an animated multi-step
   inference sequence with a pulsing neural icon (`Cpu`):
   - Step 1: "Analyzing road conditions..." (checks slope, paving ratio, mud hazards).
   - Step 2: "Analyzing weather..." (checks IMD rainfall radar, black ice, river flow).
   - Step 3: "Analyzing accessibility..." (evaluates RDAI district isolation index).
   - Step 4: "Calculating optimal route..." (synthesizes multi-modal switches and waypoints).

4. Decision Logic & Multi-Modal Switching:
   - Drone Trigger: If cargo is "Cold-Chain Vaccines" AND "Permit VTOL Drone" is checked,
     the solver selects the Garuda SkyWay Autonomous VTOL flight (e.g. 2h 15m vs 14h road).
   - Waterway Trigger: If "Enable NW-2 Barge" is checked AND destination is Dhubri or Tawang,
     the solver routes bulk cargo via river barge to bypass vulnerable highway chokepoints.
   - Heavy Road Trigger: If cargo is heavy rations or fuel, the solver selects reinforced
     6x6 off-road routes avoiding active landslide sectors.

5. Output Results:
   - Primary Recommended Route: Shows title, transit time, total distance, risk score (/100),
     fuel savings percentage, elevation profile, animated route map, and step-by-step itinerary.
   - Alternative Pathway Comparison: Compares the recommended route against a traditional
     direct highway, explicitly stating friction alerts and potential delay penalties.
   - Deploy Mission Convoy: Formally commits the route and generates a mission order.

12. ACCESSIBILITY INTELLIGENCE
================================================================================
1. The Remote District Accessibility Index (RDAI):
   - A composite logistical vulnerability scoring model (`src/data/districts.js`)
     measuring physical isolation across North Eastern border districts.
   - Score Range: 0 to 100 (Lower score = more isolated, higher supply risk).

2. Accessibility Tiers:
   - Critical Isolation (Score < 45): e.g., Kurung Kumey (Arunachal Pradesh - 38).
   - Severe Constraint (Score 45 - 59): e.g., Dima Hasao (Assam - 54), Saiha (Mizoram - 48).
   - Moderate Friction (Score 60 - 74): e.g., Mon (Nagaland - 62), West Sikkim (68).
   - Accessible (Score >= 75): e.g., Kamrup Metropolitan / Guwahati (Assam - 88).

3. Underlying Evaluated Factors:
   - Terrain Complexity: Slope angle, hair-pin frequency, elevation gradient (0-100).
   - Weather Vulnerability: Annual rainfall intensity, landslide susceptibility (0-100).
   - Bridge Redundancy: Alternate crossing points if primary bridge fails (0-100).
   - Road Bitumen Ratio: Percentage of paved all-weather roads vs. unpaved dirt tracks.
   - Nearest Air / Drone Hub Distance (km).
   - Average Transit Days to State Hub.
   - Critical Lifeline: Key bridges, aqueducts, or ridge cuts keeping the district supplied.
   - Key Supplies Stock Buffer (Days): Emergency food, medicine, and fuel reserve capacity.

4. Interactive Monsoon Flood Stress-Test Simulator:
   - A toggle on the Accessibility page simulates severe monsoon conditions across NER.
   - When activated, weather-vulnerable districts dynamically lose 9 to 16 points,
     downgrading their accessibility tiers and flagging immediate buffer stock emergencies.

20. SERVICES & BUSINESS LOGIC
================================================================================
Located in `src/services/`:

1. `api.js`:
   - `simulateDelay(ms = 150)`: Returns a promise resolving after specified milliseconds.
   - `storage`: Provides safe `get(key, fallback)` and `set(key, value)` methods wrapping
     `window.localStorage` with error handling.

2. `alertService.js`:
   - `getAllAlerts()`: Fetches all alerts, defaulting to `ALERTS` dataset.
   - `getAllIncidents()`: Fetches all incident triage records, defaulting to `INCIDENTS`.
   - `broadcastSosAlert(newAlert)`: Generates a new alert object (`ALT-xxxx`), marks status
     'Active', prepends to stored alerts array in `localStorage`, and returns it.
   - `resolveIncident(incidentId)`: Updates incident clearance progress to 100% and marks status 'Resolved'.

3. `reportService.js`:
   - `getAllReports()`: Fetches crowdsourced field reports from `localStorage` or initial mock data.
   - `submitReport(newReport)`: Creates new report (`RPT-xxx`) with initial 1 vote, timestamp 'Just now',
     stores it in `localStorage`, and prepends to the feed.
   - `upvoteReport(id)`: Increments report vote count by 1. Automatically sets `verified: true`
     if vote count reaches 10.

4. `routeService.js`:
   - `getAllRoutes()`: Returns list of strategic corridors.
   - `getRouteById(id)`: Finds corridor matching ID.
   - `calculateOptimalPath(origin, destination, options)`: Matches corridor by origin/destination
     substring, cross-references active hazards from `ALERTS`, calculates efficiency gains
     (34% with waterways, 12% without), and returns recommended route and alternatives.

5. `vehicleService.js`:
   - `getAllVehicles()`: Fetches fleet array from `localStorage` or `VEHICLES`.
   - `getVehicleById(id)`: Finds vehicle matching ID.
   - `updateTelemetry(id, telemetryUpdates)`: Merges updated speed/temp/fuel into vehicle record,
     sets `lastPing: 'Just now'`, and persists to storage.
   - `registerVehicle(newVehicle)`: Assigns unique ID (`NER-TRK-xxx`), prepends to fleet list,
     and saves to storage.

25. ERROR HANDLING & LOADING STATES
================================================================================

     informative amber banner when connectivity is lost.

     if storage is full or disabled in private browsing mode.

   - React Router configuration includes `<Route path="*" element={<Navigate to="/" replace />} />`
     to redirect unknown URLs back to the dashboard.

   - `MapView.jsx` executes `map.invalidateSize()` after mount to prevent gray unrendered tile blocks.
5. Form Validation:
   - All forms enforce HTML5 required constraints and provide user feedback.
